Microfinance & Field Loan Assessment Tablet (កម្មវិធីវាយតម្លៃឥណទាន និងទ្រព្យបញ្ចាំចល័ត)
Target Users: Credit officers (COs), pawnshop appraisers, and community financial agents.
Core Problem: Credit officers in provincial areas must assess collateral, calculate debt-service ratios, and verify household cash flow while on-site with borrowers.
Key Tablet Features:
Repayment Schedule Generator: Live interactive loan amortization schedule (flat rate, declining balance) calculating principal and interest in Khmer Riel and USD.
Collateral Photo Vault: Tag GPS-stamped photos of hard/soft titles, land boundaries, or vehicles directly to the borrower profile.
100% Offline Database: Stores applications locally in Room/SQLite and syncs automatically when returning to cellular range.
Tablet Advantage: Complex multi-year repayment amortization tables with 24–36 rows are unreadable on phones but display cleanly on tablets.
